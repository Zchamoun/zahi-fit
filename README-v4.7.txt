ZAHI FIT v4.7.0 — YOUTUBE DEMOS + VISUAL STEP-BY-STEP (NO MORE IMAGES)

WHAT'S NEW
- Every exercise's "How to do it" now opens:
  1) A YouTube demo video that plays inside the app (tap the orange play button).
     55 of the 58 exercises have a chosen technique video from established coaching
     channels (NASM, Ask Doctor Jo, Squat University, ScottHerman, Runna, Tom Morrison…).
     "Open in YouTube" opens the same video in the YouTube app.
     The two circuits and Walk/Jog show "Find a video on YouTube" instead.
  2) A simple visual step-by-step guide: five steps, each with an icon (📍 set up,
     🛡️ brace, ⬇️ lower, ⬆️ drive up, ⏸️ hold, 🔁 repeat…). Tap a step to open it.
     "Talk me through it" reads each step aloud and highlights it as it goes.
     Works in English and German, and offline (videos need internet).
- "Change video": don't like a video, or it stops working? Paste any YouTube link and it's
  used from then on on this phone. "Use the recommended video" switches back.
- The exercise screen shows the video's preview picture on the "How to do it" button.
- All exercise photos and illustrations have been removed.
