ZAHI FIT v4.2.0 — GERMAN EXERCISES + CONFIRM LANGUAGE & VOICE

WHAT'S NEW
- Choosing Deutsch now changes the whole exercise experience, not only the voice:
  exercise names, the exercise screen (targets, columns, tips, load suggestions, Technique),
  the step-by-step guide text and the spoken guide. All 40 exercises and 39 five-step guides
  are written in German inside the app.
- Choose, then Confirm: in Profile › Language & voice, pick language, voice (Male / Female /
  Neutral) and voice quality, then tap Confirm. You hear a short preview in the new voice.
- Change it from inside the guide too: tap the "EN · Female" chip at the top right of any guide.
  The guide reloads in the new language at the same step.
- German now works offline: the German text is in the app, so "Phone voice" reads it with your
  phone's German voice without internet. The natural voice no longer needs a translation step,
  so German clips are also faster.
- "Save guides for offline" (Natural only): downloads the spoken guide for every exercise in
  your plan in the confirmed language and voice, so it plays in the gym without signal.
- "How to set up your phone's voices": step-by-step phone setup inside the app.
- Includes the v4.1.1 voice fix. History and load suggestions are shared across languages.
- Menus (Today, History, Coach, Profile) stay in English for now.
