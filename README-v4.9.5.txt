ZAHI FIT v4.9.5 — FIND A REAL MALE VOICE

WHY MALE STILL SOUNDED FEMALE
Chrome gets one voice per language/accent from the phone. On this phone those voices are female,
so "Male" could only be a deeper female voice. A pitch change can't turn a female voice into a
male one — the male tone has to come from a male voice on the phone.

WHAT'S NEW (Female, Neutral and Speed unchanged)
- Profile › Language & voice › Male › "Find a male voice on this phone":
  lists every voice your phone has for the language (one per accent: US, UK, Australia, India…).
  Tap ▶ to hear each one exactly as it sounds (no pitch trick) and tap Use on one that sounds
  male. That voice is then used for Male everywhere: rest cues, encouragement, guides, Test voice.
- If no male voice has been chosen yet, an orange hint says so and opens the finder.
- The finder shows the free 2-minute fix if none sound male:
  Settings › Text-to-speech › Speech Services by Google › ⚙ › Install voice data ›
  English (United Kingdom) › pick a male voice › reopen Zahi Fit › Find a male voice › Use.
- The same finder exists for Female.
- Until a real male voice is chosen, Male is a little deeper than before.
