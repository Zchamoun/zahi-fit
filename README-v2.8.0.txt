What changed
- New full-screen Visual PT Coach that stays inside Zahi Fit.
- Large step-specific body-position illustrations with callout labels.
- Five-position visual sequences for World's Greatest Stretch and Deadlift.
- Step thumbnails plus Previous / Next controls.
- Much more detailed position, movement and technique instructions.
- “What you should feel” and common mistakes/corrections.
- Voice coaching is slower and smoother, with Very slow / Smooth / Normal speeds.
- Voice reads one step at a time instead of rushing through the entire exercise.
- Play/Pause and Replay Step controls.
- Existing v2.7 profile, age/sex context, timer sounds, embedded AI PT, planner, history and workout tracking remain intact.
- Cache bumped to v2.8.0.

Note
The v2.8 visual system includes bespoke movement illustrations for the two currently tested exercises (World's Greatest Stretch and Deadlift) and a safe illustrated fallback for other exercises. More exercise-specific visual sequences can be added without changing the interface.
