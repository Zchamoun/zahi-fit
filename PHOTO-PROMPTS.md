# Zahi Fit — exercise photo production pack

The app now shows real photos in the technique guide whenever these files exist, and falls back to the existing illustrations when they don't. Upload photos progressively; nothing breaks in between.

## Where the files go

Folder: `pt-assets-v34/` in the repository root. File name: `<family>-step1.jpg` … `<family>-step5.jpg` (exact spelling and capitals). 16:9 landscape, 1536 × 864 is ideal, JPG under ~250 KB.

## House style (paste this before every prompt)

> Photorealistic fitness instruction photo, 16:9 landscape. The same athletic man in every image: early 30s, short dark hair, trimmed dark beard, fitted black crew-neck T-shirt, black athletic shorts, black trainers with white soles, black smartwatch on left wrist. Dark charcoal textured studio wall, dark grey rubber gym floor, soft even key light from the front-left, subtle rim light. Camera at hip height, three-quarter side view, whole body and all equipment fully in frame with space around them. Matte black equipment with small blue accents. Correct, safe exercise technique. Top-left: a solid blue circle containing the white step number, followed by the step title in bold white sans-serif on a dark translucent bar. Right side: a dark navy rounded box with a thin bright-blue border containing 3–5 short white bullet points. No logos except a small generic mark on the shirt, no watermark.

Tip: generate all five steps of an exercise in one conversation so the model, lighting and camera stay identical; ask for "same person, same setup, next phase of the movement". Check the text in the bullet box for spelling before saving — or ask for the image without text; the app shows the instructions underneath anyway.
