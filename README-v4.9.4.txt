ZAHI FIT v4.9.4 — NICER MALE & FEMALE VOICES, MORE ENCOURAGING COACHING

WHY MALE/FEMALE SOUNDED WRONG
Chrome usually gets one voice per language from the phone. v4.9.3 made Male and Female by
bending that one voice's pitch a lot — which sounds robotic (male) or cartoonish (female).

FIXES (Neutral is unchanged)
- Gentler tuning: Male is moderately lower and a little slower (calm, steady); Female is slightly
  brighter (warm, not squeaky). Still clearly different from each other and from Neutral.
- Real voices first: when the phone has a real male/female voice, it's used almost unaltered.
- NEW "Voice used for Male / Female / Neutral" (Profile › Language & voice): pick any voice on
  your phone for each option. A real voice you assign is used as-is — this gives the best result.
  Tap "Show me how" for the steps to give one English accent (e.g. United Kingdom) a male voice
  in Google's settings, then assign it to Male.
- More encouraging coaching, with variety (English and German), e.g.
  "Nice work. Rest for 90 seconds." · "Ten seconds to go. You've got this." ·
  "Rest's done. Next set — you've got this." · "Great set!"

Still 100% phone voices: free, offline, no credit.
