ZAHI FIT v4.5.0 — SEVERAL PEOPLE ON ONE PHONE

WHAT'S NEW
- Each person on the phone has their own: workout history, plan, sex/age, language & voice,
  coach chat, offline voice guides status and any workout in progress.
- Tap the round initial at the top right of Today → "Who's training?" → tap a name to switch,
  or "+ Add a person".
- A new person gets the short setup ("Hi Sara, let's set up your training").
- Profile › People on this phone: rename yourself, switch, add, or delete someone
  (deleting erases only that person's data).
- "Erase data" and "Back up history" in Profile now apply to the person who's active.
- The coach knows the name of the person it's talking to.

YOUR EXISTING DATA
Nothing moves. Your current history, plan and settings become the first person ("Me").
Rename yourself in Profile › People on this phone.

NOTE
Switching isn't password-protected: it keeps everyone's data separate and tidy, not private
from someone holding the phone.
