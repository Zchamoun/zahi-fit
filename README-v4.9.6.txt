ZAHI FIT v4.9.6 — VOICE SETUP GUIDE FOR ANDROID AND IPHONE

WHAT'S NEW
- Profile › Language & voice › "How to set up your phone's voices" now has two tabs:
  • Android (Samsung, Google Pixel and others): install Speech Recognition and Synthesis from
    Google › make it the Preferred engine › Install voice data › pick a male or female voice for
    English (United Kingdom) and Deutsch (Deutschland) › Close all › reopen › Find a voice › Use.
  • iPhone / iPad: Settings › Accessibility › Spoken Content › Voices › download English and German
    voices (e.g. Daniel/Arthur male UK, Samantha female US, Martin male German, Anna female German)
    › reopen › Find a voice › Use. Includes a "No sound?" tip (Silent mode).
  The guide opens on the right tab for the phone automatically; tap the other tab any time.
- "Find a male / female voice" shows the short steps for the user's phone, plus a link to the
  full guide.
- iPhone voice names (Daniel, Arthur, Aaron, Evan, Nathan, Martin… / Samantha, Ava, Zoe, Kate,
  Anna, Helena…) are recognised as male or female automatically.
