ZAHI FIT v4.3.0 — CONSISTENT SAVE / CONFIRM / TEST BUTTONS

One rule across Profile:
- TEAL = there's something to save, confirm or test.
- GREY = saved / up to date.

Your plan ........ grey "Plan saved" → change anything → teal "Save plan" → tap → grey again.
About you ........ grey "Saved" → change sex or age → teal "Save" → tap → grey again.
                   (Changing it back to what's saved turns it grey again.)
Language & voice . grey "Confirmed" → change language, voice or quality → teal "Confirm" → tap → grey.
Test voice ....... teal until you've heard the current voice, then grey "Test voice again".
                   Turns teal again whenever you confirm a new voice or change the speed.
Save guides ...... teal until the guides are saved for your current language and voice, then grey
                   "Guides saved for offline ✓". Teal again after you change language or voice.

Includes everything from v4.2 (German exercises and guide, confirm flow, offline save).

UPLOAD (repository root, commit to main)
Replace/add all files in this folder: app.js, data-de.js, index.html, styles.css, sw.js, README-v4.3.txt
(If v4.2 is already uploaded, only app.js, styles.css and sw.js actually changed.)
