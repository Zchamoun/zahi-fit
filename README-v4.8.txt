ZAHI FIT v4.8.0 — PLANS TAILORED TO THE PERSON + DAILY CALIBRATION

1) YOUR PLAN IS BUILT FOR YOU
New setup questions (onboarding and Profile › About you):
  • Sex, age (existing)            • Training experience: new / some / experienced
  • Body weight (optional, kg)     • Focus area: Auto (by sex), Balanced, Legs & glutes, Upper body, Core
  • Joints to go easy on or pain areas: knees, lower back, shoulders, hips, wrists, elbows, neck, ankles

How they shape the plan:
  • Sex — Auto focus: women get extra glute & leg work, men extra upper-body work (pick a focus to
    change this). Sex also sets starting weights and exercise versions.
  • Age — from 50: longer warm-up, fewer jumps; from 60: balance work, joint-friendly versions
    (e.g. leg press instead of front squat), no very heavy low-rep sets, a little more rest.
  • Experience — new: fewer sets, simpler versions of technical lifts (e.g. kettlebell deadlift);
    experienced: an extra set on the main lifts.
  • Pain areas — exercises that load that area are swapped for gentler options automatically.
  • Session length — new 45-minute option; sessions are capped at a realistic number of exercises.
Saving About you rebuilds your plan straight away.

2) EVERY SESSION IS CALIBRATED TO HOW YOU FEEL
The check-in (energy, soreness, time: 45 / 60 / 75 / 90 min) sets today's level:
  • Fresh (high energy, not sore): small step up.
  • Planned: as written.
  • Lighter day (low energy or sore): ~7% lighter, one fewer accessory set, stop 3–4 reps short,
    longer rests, easier cardio.
  • Recovery day (very tired or very sore): ~15% lighter, fewer sets, no jumping, easy cardio.
Every exercise now shows "Today: 3 × 8 @ 62.5 kg · rest 2:00" with the reason.
  • Weight comes from your last session (up if it felt easy, down if very hard) or, the first
    time, a starting estimate from your profile ("adjust after your first set").
  • 45-minute sessions pair the accessory with core back-to-back to save time.
  • The weight box is pre-filled with today's target; one tap on ✓ logs it.
The AI coach also receives your full profile and today's target.

Note: pain-area swaps are a guide, not medical advice. Sharp, worsening or lasting pain: stop
and get it checked by a physio or doctor.
